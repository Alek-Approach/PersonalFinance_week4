Part 3: Reflection and Sharing

5 minutes

Case Study: Choosing the Best Retirement Account

Group activity

Goal: Work with your group to decide the best retirement account for a hypothetical person based on their details.

Steps:

  1. Read your scenario and discuss the person’s age, income, and financial situation.
  2. Review retirement account options (401(k), Roth 401(k), IRA, Roth IRA, HSA).
  3. Decide which account fits their needs best and why.
  4. Prepare a 1-minute explanation to share with the class, including:
    • The person’s key details.
    • Your recommended account and its benefits.
